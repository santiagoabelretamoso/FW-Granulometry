A single workspace

From raw grain-size data
to interpretable plots.

FW-Granulometry organizes the granulometric workflow in one graphical interface. Combine sieve and laser data, build hybrid distributions, produce plots and extend the interpretation with componentry analysis.

01

Sieve

Load sieve data in φ classes from Excel and calculate granulometric parameters for individual samples or groups.

Coarse fractions · φ scale
02

Laser

Import laser diffraction measurements in μm, convert them to the granulometric framework and integrate the fine fraction into the project.

Fine fractions · μm → φ
03

Hybrid

Combine sieve and laser data from the same sample to build a unified distribution across coarse and fine fractions.

Depositional interpretation

Discrimination diagrams inside the Graphic workflow.

Plot samples on mean grain-size versus sorting diagrams and compare them with published depositional fields. Graphic keeps sample points, field boundaries and labels editable in the same figure workflow.

Flow, Surge, Surge Dunes and Fall fields are shown here in the same visual logic as the Graphic output.

Componentry lives inside the project.

FW-Granulometry 2.0 can connect granulometric distributions with componentry data. Work by fraction, classify particles, inspect compositions and preserve the connection to the source sample.

Santiago Abel Retamoso is a geologist and volcanologist. He is currently pursuing a PhD in Geological Sciences at the National University of Salta (Salta, Argentina) and is based at the Bariloche Atomic Centre (Río Negro, Argentina). He is also completing a Diploma in Data Science at the National Technological University (Buenos Aires, Argentina). His research focuses on silica-rich explosive phreatomagmatic eruptions in continental settings.

FW-Granulometry was developed as a scientific tool for working with grain-size and componentry data from tephra and sedimentary deposits. Its aim is to facilitate the processing and visualization of robust datasets within a single workflow.
